# What is a Box

A **Box** is the physical unit of storing and shipping goods in Seller Exchange.
When an [Order](003-order.en.md) is fulfilled, the purchased
[Products](001-product.en.md) are packed into boxes; those boxes are received at the
warehouse and later grouped for shipment.

## Overview

If a Product is *what* you sell and an Order is *the request to buy it*, a Box is
*where the physical goods actually sit*. Each box holds products, belongs to an
order, and carries the details needed to move it: its destination and its
logistics tariff. Boxes are the atoms of warehouse work.

## Why it matters

Warehouse operations revolve around boxes. Receiving, editing, splitting, merging
and redistributing all happen at the box level, and shipments to Amazon are
assembled from boxes. Accurate boxes mean accurate stock and smooth shipping.

## Box operations

Boxes can be created, edited, grouped, merged and redistributed. Related boxes
can be combined into a **Super Box** — a container of boxes (shown as **SB**).

> A Super Box is a confirmed grouping concept (a container of boxes). It is not
> yet documented as a standalone entity with its own card and statuses, so treat
> it as a way of grouping boxes rather than a separate object.

## Lifecycle and statuses (on the warehouse)

A box moves through the warehouse along this path:

> NEW (in transit to the China prep centre) → ACCEPTED_IN_PROCESSING → IN_STOCK →
> REQUESTED_SEND_TO_BATCH → IN_BATCH → IN_BATCH_ON_THE_WAY → FINISH_PREP_CENTR_USA

The exact status values are maintained in
[Entity States → warehouse / shipment statuses](../../ENTITY_STATES.md).

## How it relates to other concepts

- A Box **contains** Products and **belongs to** an Order.
- It is **processed by** a Warehouse Task and **references** a Destination and a
  Tariff; it is **stored in** a Warehouse.
- Boxes are grouped into a **[Batch](005-batch.md)** (and can be grouped into a
  Super Box).

> Whether **Shipment** is a separate entity or simply the warehouse lifecycle of
> a box is still being confirmed (Needs validation). Relationship details are in
> [Entity Relationships](../../ENTITY_RELATIONSHIPS.md).
